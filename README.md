## Series Summary
High school chemistry teacher Walter White turns to manufacturing methamphetamine after a cancer diagnosis, transforming into ruthless drug kingpin "Heisenberg."

## Main Characters
- **Walter White** - Teacher → Drug Lord
- **Jesse Pinkman** - Partner & Protégé  
- **Skyler White** - Wife
- **Hank Schrader** - DEA Agent
- **Gus Fring** - Rival Kingpin
- **Saul Goodman** - Lawyer

## Key Episodes

- **"Face Off"** - Gus Fring's death
- **"Ozymandias"** - Series climax (Highest rated)
- **"Felina"** - Perfect finale
